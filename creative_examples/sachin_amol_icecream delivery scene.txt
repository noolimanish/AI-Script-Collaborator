[REGISTER: slice-of-life comedy / friendship]
[VIBE: lonely celebration, quiet realism, hyper-logical banter, no romance]

**INT. AMOL'S FLAT - ENTRANCE - NIGHT**

A half-open wooden door. 

SACHIN (22) stands outside in the corridor wearing a faded delivery polo and a worn-out helmet still strapped tightly under his chin. He holds a chilled insulated bag in both hands. 

His phone pings from his pocket—the frantic chime of a delivery app timer.

AMOL (23) opens the door fully. Loose oversized gym shorts, bare feet, hair sticking up on one side. He’s holding a plastic fork.

SACHIN
Naturals roasted almond, two tubs. Bhayya, delivery OTP chepthe...

AMOL
(eyes lighting up)
Haa! Ochava! Bhai, cheppulu vidichi lopaliki ra.

SACHIN
Ledhu bro, parcel teesko. OTP cheppu chaalu, next pickup Srinagar Colony lo undi.

AMOL
(already pulling him by the wrist)
Arre daa ra babu, bayata humidity chusava ela undho? AC on lo undi. Rendu nimishalu kurchunte evadu em anadu. Raa.

SACHIN
(resisting slightly)
Bhayya... duty lo unna bhayya. Penalty padthadi.

AMOL
Oka five minutes ki evadu terminate cheyyadu. Raa ante.

Amol literally pulls Sachin inside by the elbow and kicks the door shut with his heel. 

*CLICK.* The latch falls into place.

**INT. AMOL'S LIVING ROOM - CONTINUOUS**

The room is messy in a distinctly bachelor way. A ceiling fan wobbles at full speed. On the center table sits a half-cut chocolate truffle cake in a crushed cardboard box, surrounded by empty paper plates.

Sachin stands awkwardly in the middle of the room, hugging his delivery bag to his chest like a bulletproof vest. He hasn't taken off his helmet.

SACHIN
Bro, serious ga chepthunna... app lo location freeze aypothadi. OTP cheppu please.

AMOL
Mundhu helmet theeyi bhayya. Astronaut laaga unnav hall lo. Kurcho akkada.

Amol gestures to a single-seater sofa. Sachin, not wanting to trigger him, carefully sits on the extreme edge of the cushion.

Amol disappears into the kitchen. 

Sachin pulls out his phone. The delivery app flashes: *“Customer location reached. Enter 4-digit PIN.”* 

SACHIN
(calling out)
Bhayya! 

AMOL (O.S.)
Vesthunna! Aagu!

Amol walks back in holding a steel plate with a massive, uneven slab of cake. A spoon is stuck vertically into the center of it. He shoves the plate right into Sachin's lap.

SACHIN
(alarmed)
Idi nenu order ivvaledhu bro. Ice cream eh thecha nenu.

AMOL
Nenu pettanu neeku. Thinu.

SACHIN
Naku sweets padav bhayya. Cold chesthadi.

AMOL
Chocolate cake thinte cold chesthada? Em logic bhayya needhi? Thinu, genuine bakery cake. Fresh.

SACHIN
Nen ippude tiffin chesa...

AMOL
(staring at him, dead serious)
Bhayya, naatho thintava leda?

Sachin looks at the cake, then at Amol’s unblinking eyes. He cautiously takes the spoon, scrapes off a tiny, transparent crumb, and puts it in his mouth.

SACHIN
Good. Baavundi. Ippudu OTP—

AMOL
Inka pedda piece theesko.

Sachin sighs, takes a proper bite, and chews miserably.

AMOL (CONT'D)
Celebration.

SACHIN
(muffled)
Birthday ah?

AMOL
Kaadhu. GATE qualify ayya.

Sachin stops chewing. He looks up.

SACHIN
GATE ah? Mechanical ah?

AMOL
Computer Science. Third attempt. 

SACHIN
Oh. Nice. Rank entha vachindi?

AMOL
(beat)
Rank adagaku bhayya. Manam qualify ayyindi boundary lo. Cut-off twenty-seven unte... twenty-seven point six vachindi. But still... qualified is qualified, right?

Sachin looks at the miserable slice of cake, then at the empty living room. No friends. No banners. Just the hum of the fridge.

SACHIN
Party cheskodaniki evaru raledha?

AMOL
(scratching his neck)
Roommates unnaru. Okadiki night shift. Inkokadu gadi girlfriend tho cinema ki velladu. Vadu vellipothu ‘Cake techam ga, thini paduko’ annadu. Nene bore kotti ice cream order petta... evaranna vastharu kada ani.

Sachin’s phone buzzes violently. A notification pops up: *“Batch order reassigned due to inactivity.”*

Sachin looks at the screen. The urgent timer disappears. The penalty is already done. 

He stares at the blank screen for two seconds. 

He slowly unclips his helmet strap. He pulls the helmet off and places it on the floor beside his shoes. His hair is matted flat with sweat.

SACHIN
Twenty-seven point six ah?

AMOL
Avunu.

SACHIN
Nenu second year lo crack cheddam ani Ace Academy lo join ayya.

AMOL
(surprised)
Nuvva? Engineering ah?

SACHIN
ECE. First month lo Digital Logic class ki velli... K-map chusi bayapadi paripoya. Inka vellale. Fee motham bokka.

Amol lets out a loud, sudden snort of laughter. 

AMOL
K-map ah? Rey, K-map chala easy ra!

SACHIN
Easy na bongu. Aame board meeda round lu geesthunte naku kallu thirigipoyevi. Ma nanna emo repu పొద్దున్నే PSU job kottesthadu ani colony lo cheppesukunnadu. 

AMOL
(grinning, sitting on the coffee table opposite him)
Maa nanna aithe direct ga IIT Kharagpur brochure fridge meeda magnet tho pettadu. Chusthe cutoff kante rendu marks ekkuva vachindi. Final ga em vachindi? Ee cake vachindi.

Sachin looks at the cake on his plate. He takes a bigger bite without being asked.

SACHIN
Cream baane undi le.

AMOL
Naturals roasted almond baguntadi kada?

SACHIN
Avunu, melting fast. Open cheyyi, thindham.

Amol quickly pulls the cold insulated bag toward himself, ripping open the zipper. He grabs two plastic spoons from the TV unit.

AMOL
OTP cheppana ippudu?

SACHIN
(chewing)
Order cancel aypoyindi le. Five minutes tharvatha cheppu. First aa ice cream theeyi.
