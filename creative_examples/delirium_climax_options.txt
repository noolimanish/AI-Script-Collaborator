END_1
Opening Foreshadow (Ambulance)

Film starts with ambulance siren outside Arjun’s house.

He casually thinks: “Maybe something happened to a tenant.”

Audience forgets it… but it connects later.

Main Story Plays Out

(Dream doctor, friend taking him, watch stuck at 11:00, etc — all as you wrote earlier.)

Hospital Awakening

Arjun opens his eyes. A real doctor is standing there.

He’s weak, confused.

Arjun: “Where’s my friend? Where am I? Did I stop dreaming… or am I still inside one?”

Doctor gives a warm smile.

Doctor: “You were in a coma for ten days. Your mother brought you here. A ceiling fan fell on you while you were sleeping. That’s why you were here.”

Doctor adds:

Doctor: “It’s common to dream while in coma. Don’t think too much — everything is normal now.”

False Comfort

Arjun breathes with relief. He looks at the wall clock — it’s ticking normally, not stuck at 11:00.

He smiles slightly.

Final Suspense Hit

Suddenly, he notices someone sitting in the chair opposite him.

It’s the friend — the same one from his dream.

The friend stares silently at Arjun… no expression.

Arjun panics, calls out:

Arjun: “Mom! Can you see someone here?”

From outside the curtain, his mother replies:

Mother (O.S.): “No, why? Is everything okay?”

Arjun turns back — the friend is still sitting there, staring.

CUT TO BLACK.



END__2
2. Doppelgänger Ending (He’s Not Alone)

Hospital scene plays out, doctor explains coma, mother reassures.

Arjun finally calms down, smiles.

Then… another Arjun walks into the room.

Same clothes, same look — but cold expression.

Dream-Arjun and Real-Arjun stare at each other.

Doctor & mother react to only one Arjun.

Which means… either the dream Arjun escaped into reality, or Arjun himself is still dreaming.

One Arjun smiles sinisterly, while the other looks terrified.

CUT TO BLACK.

Audience: “Wait… which Arjun survived? Which is real??”


END__3
3. The Observer Ending (Bigger Twist)

Arjun wakes up in hospital, thinks it’s all over.

He’s relieved, but notices a man in the corner with a camera, writing notes.

Arjun asks, “Who’s that?”

Doctor & mother say: “What? There’s no one.”

The man (Observer) keeps writing. He looks directly at Arjun and whispers:

Observer: “You’re not supposed to wake up yet.”

Suddenly, everything glitches — walls, doctor, mother, all start flickering like a simulation.

Arjun screams: “Am I still dreaming?!”

CUT TO BLACK with loud heartbeat sound.

END__4
“Safe… Until the Twist” Ending

1. Calm Resolution

Arjun wakes up in the hospital.

Doctor & mother reassure him — “You were in a coma, everything is fine now.”

Arjun smiles, relieved, finally breathing freely.

He checks the clock → it’s normal, not 11:00.

He calls his mother:

ARJUN: “Mom… everything okay now?”

MOTHER: “Yes, beta. You’re fine. Come home soon.”

He leaves the hospital with his mother or friend, everything seems normal.

He rides his bike back home. Sun is shining, birds chirping.

Audience thinks: “Okay, story done. He’s safe. End of nightmare.”

2. Subtle Suspense (Foreshadow)

Arjun enters his home.

Everything seems fine: his room, furniture, clock ticking normally.

He sits on the bed, relaxes, looks out the window — peaceful morning vibe.

He laughs quietly to himself: “Finally… back to normal.”

Camera holds on him for a few seconds. Calm BGM.

3. Sudden Twist (Unexpected)

Arjun’s phone vibrates. He picks it up.

Message on the screen:

“You’re still dreaming. Wake up.”

Confused, he drops the phone.

Camera pans to the wall clock → hands spin backward for a second, then stop at 11:00.

He hears a faint whisper:

“Did you really think it was over?”

Slowly, shadow of Arjun 2 appears in the corner, smiling eerily.

Arjun freezes.

CUT TO BLACK.
