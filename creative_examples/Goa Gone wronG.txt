Goa Gone Wrong
Three friends were planning to go to Goa, and their wish came true.
They were travelling to Goa in a train. They reached Goa with a lot of excitement and happiness on their faces. One by one, they were getting down from different compartments. They were ready to take a room and stay somewhere.
They stayed and went to the beach, and there they saw a man on the beach who was sitting alone. The 1st friend saw him suspiciously as he was wearing a face mask on the beach, with one cloth/bandage (keera) over one eye and the other open. The 1st friend looked at him multiple times. This beachman peeped with one eye suddenly, and when they saw him, he closed his eyes.
They left him and went away from there. After some time, they again saw him in that place, again on the next bench beside them. He was sleeping on it, slowly peeping from over a magazine on his face.
They saw this confusedly. Then suddenly, at their cottage, a man peeped from the window. They got afraid, and slowly one of the friends caught a rod (wooden). Suddenly, when he turned back—revealing that face mask on his face—the friend feared and hit him on his head!

They see him on the ground. The 3 friends are in shock and fear about what's happening, because he is not getting up. No matter how much they try to wake him up, he is not responding. One of the friends tries to hear his heartbeat. In shock, they find no heartbeat. The 3 friends were even more scared now about what happened to that guy. Did he die?
Now they try to take him in and try to hide him in one place. Before they can think of a place, suddenly the sound of a gate opening comes. They see the owner of the building is coming, so they carry the dead body as if he was alive. The owner sees them and gets confused about what happened to him. They say he is drunk and so couldn't walk correctly.
The owner tries to talk to him and give him a life lesson on how and when to drink, and about drinking responsibly. Behind that guy, they try to manage this guy. Suddenly, the owner turns back. They cover it up as if he is puking.
The owner also runs over and catches his arm and rubs it, as he feels a little cold, and gives him some tablets to make him sober. Now the task is to make him take the tablet without the owner getting suspicious. After that, the owner leaves and says bye to that guy.
Now these friends, they want to do something so that they will get out of this situation. Now they try to bury him, so they do different plans to take him out of the house and put him somewhere. They take him to a forest place where no one is there. They put him in a beach-forest area. They dump him and come towards the main road. Everyone was tense, but one guy was talking to someone. They think he's mom, but the 2 friends observe he laughs and says "hi," and they get shocked. He's chilling with his girlfriend on a call. (Angry 2 friends).
While they were on the road, one friend gets fear as it was an empty and dark place. He fears for a ghost, as they killed someone. Suddenly, the afraid friend walks under a banyan tree, and suddenly a white cloth comes and falls on him. That friend under the cloth gets blank and in fear, he runs. Seeing that guy in the white cloth, the 2nd friend also runs, but still, the third friend sits and talks.
The 1st friend under the scarf was running, going faster than the 2nd friend. Then he gets a doubt: why is the ghost running faster than me? He sees the dress under the scarf, and then he removes the scarf. 1st friend now realizes how he will be able to see lights, and he thinks friend 2 saved him. And friend 2 hits him on the head and explains that the scarf came from the tree, which was tied for some belief, by the wind. "You realized that and saved us also." But then they realize the 3rd friend was missing. They look here and there, and they find he's talking to his girlfriend even in this worst situation. They scold him and they leave for home. (We can only see they left for home).
They came back home and sat down to pack their bags to go back, and they hear a gate sound continuously. All of them check from the window. One guy in a sweater, looking scary, is playing on the gate. Suddenly they get fear, as if the ghost came back—the one who they killed. They rush to the bedroom and lock it in a hurry. They forget to lock the main room.
Friend 1 remembers and peeps out of the house. The same TV sound was coming, and he was watching TV. Suddenly, he closes the door and thinks, and asks his other friends, "Does a ghost watch movies? Because he is watching Go Goa Gone."
Suddenly, the 3rd friend gets a doubt as he was peeping to where the 2 friends were talking. He asks, "Hey, do you guys know how my slippers came back home? You guys picked them?" These 2 friends ask, "Why, what did you wear then?" He tells them he liked that guy's shoes, so he gave him his slippers as a gift and took them. 😊
Now they get clarification that he came back, but the 1st & 3rd friends get a doubt because he was dead. Both look towards the 2nd friend and ask, "Now, how did you confirm he was dead?" He says, "I didn't hear his heartbeat." The 1st friend asks, "Which side did you hear?" Slowly after reimagining, he figures out, "Right side."
Slowly all peep out to confirm whether he is alive or a ghost, but he was missing from the hall. The TV is playing. Suddenly, they hear the sounds of a knife in the kitchen. They think he was trying to kill them, so they were ready, catching the same rod before they hit him.
He saw them afraid and says, "Hey, what happened? Everyone got tense. I made an omelet for you guys. Let's watch a movie." So they sit and watch the movie together and ask him, "Why were you following us all the time?"
"I don't have anyone here. You were looking like a 'Go Goa Gone' movie cast, so I wanted to join you guys and enjoy Goa. 3 friends see him with a look. He says, "Really, what to do? I don't have friends." They seem to talk and laugh and watch the movie.
