**INT. DINING ROOM - NIGHT**

A four-seater dining table with a faded floral plastic sheet. 

The ceiling fan wobbles slightly on speed three, ticking with every rotation: *tik... tik... tik.*

In the hallway corner, a grey VIP trolley bag stands upright. A backpack leans against it, zipped shut.

VENKAT (58) sits at the head of the table in a white banian and lungi. His reading glasses rest low on the bridge of his nose. He methodically mixes rice and tomato rasam on his steel plate, fingers working in small, quiet circles.

Opposite him sits VASU (25), wearing an old college t-shirt. His plate is barely touched. He breaks a papad into tiny, unnecessary pieces, piling the crumbs into a neat pyramid at the edge of his plate.

SAVITRI (52) emerges from the kitchen with a small steel bowl of curd. The hem of her saree is tucked at the waist. She sets the bowl between them, glancing between the two men.

SAVITRI
(low voice)
Annam inkonchem veyyana, Vasu?

VASU
Chalu amma. 

Vasu picks up the glass of water. Sips. Sets it down in the exact ring of moisture it left behind.

Venkat doesn’t look up. He reaches out for the curd bowl. His hand is steady, sun-spotted, knuckles thick from decades of desk work. He scoops two spoons of curd onto his rice.

VENKAT
Morning enni gantlaku train?

Vasu hesitates for half a second.

VASU
Six-forty. Secunderabad nunchi.

Venkat chews slowly. He takes a piece of lemon pickle from the rim of his plate, taps the excess oil against the edge, and places it on his tongue.

VENKAT
Platform two meedhaki vasthadi. Foot-over-bridge meedha escalator eppudu pani cheyadhu akkada. Luggage ekkuva undha?

VASU
Okkate bag. Heavy em ledhu.

VENKAT
Sare.

Silence returns. The fan ticks. *Tik... tik... tik.*

Savitri wipes the edge of the dining table with a damp cloth, lingering near Vasu’s chair.

SAVITRI
Morning tea ki lesthara? Five ki stove veligistha...

VENKAT
(cutting in, calm)
Auto dorakadhu antha early ga. App lo book chesthe drop out avtharu last minute lo. 

VASU
Main road varaku nadisthe share autos untay nanna. Nenu chuskunta.

Venkat’s fingers pause in his rice. Just for two beats. 

He doesn’t argue. He resumes mixing, scraping the bottom of the plate with the side of his thumb.

VENKAT
City vere ayna colony okate untadi anukoku. Bangalore lo language thelikapothe auto vadu chuttu thippi double theeskuntadu.

VASU
Office cab facility undi le. Flatmates kuda akkade untaru.

Venkat swallows. He wipes his hand against the side of his plate, then takes a sip of water. His eyes glance towards the hallway—at the VIP trolley bag—and then drop back to his plate.

VENKAT
Offer letter hard copy theeskellava?

VASU
Mail lo undi.

VENKAT
Printout theeyalsindi. Phone battery aypothe platform meedha evadiki chupisthav?

VASU
Company security daggara mail chupisthe saripothadi. Physical copy evaru adagaru ippudu.

Venkat stares at his fingers for a moment. He doesn't look convinced, but he lets it go.

VENKAT
Savitri, manchi neellu aypoyay.

Savitri quickly steps forward with the steel jug, filling his tumbler to the brim.

Venkat finishes the last bite. He pushes his chair back, the metal legs scraping loudly against the mosaic tiles. He stands, walks to the washbasin in the corner, and washes his hands. 

The tap water rushes into the sink, breaking the quiet. He dries his hands on the towel hanging by the nail, taking his time with each finger.

Vasu looks down at his plate. The food is cold. He quietly gathers the papad crumbs and pushes the plate slightly forward.

Venkat turns off the washbasin light. He walks past the dining table towards the hallway, his slippers slapping gently against the floor.

He stops beside the VIP trolley bag.

He looks down at it.

The top zipper isn't closed fully—about two inches remain open, showing the corner of a folded shirt.

Venkat stands there in the dim hall light. 

He bends down slowly, his knees making a faint crack. With two fingers, he takes the small zipper pull and slides it all the way to the end, tucking the metal tab firmly into its slot.

He straightens up, adjusts his lungi, and walks into his bedroom, leaving the door half-open.
