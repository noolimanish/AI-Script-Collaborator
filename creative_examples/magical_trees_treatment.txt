The Magical Trees
1. Into the Deep Forest
High up on a serene hill station surrounded by dense forests lived a hardworking, simple woodcutter. Though poor, he lived a content life, earning his living by cutting wood and selling it in the city.
One day, he received a massive bulk order for timber. To fulfill the large requirement, he ventured deeper into a remote part of the forest—a place he had never explored before. Evening was already setting in, and because of the sheer quantity of wood needed, he had to make three separate trips with his truck to haul everything back home.
2. Strange Encounters
During his first trip, he heard faint, unusual sounds echoing through the trees. Thinking it was just a wild animal, he ignored them and continued loading the wood. Suddenly, he heard a woman screaming for terror-filled help. Terrified and adrenaline-pumping, he dropped his tools and ran toward the direction of the voice.
In the distance, a strange light glowed. He crept forward cautiously, thinking it might be a vehicle, but as the light moved closer, he realized something horrifying: the illumination was coming from the glowing eyes of mysterious, humanoid figures.
Peeking from his hiding spot, he watched in utter disbelief. These beings wielded high-end, futuristic weapons and were abducting ordinary people, taking them into a bizarre, unreal treehouse-like structure hidden among the foliage. Then, just as mysteriously as they appeared, the trees shifted, moving underground, and the entire forest returned to normal.
3. The Impossible Home
Shaken and frightened, the woodcutter crept back to where he had parked his truck—only to find it completely gone. Panicked, he sprinted all the way back to his house on foot, desperate to find his vehicle.
To his shock, his truck was parked right outside his home. But when he peered inside, he saw someone else inside the house—someone who looked exactly like him.
Hiding in the shadows, he watched in horror as the mysterious glowing-eyed people walked into his house and handed his double the exact same bulk wood order he had received earlier that day. Confusion and dread washed over him. “They gave me this order yesterday... why are they giving it again?”
Suddenly, the realization hit him like a lightning bolt: Today is yesterday. He was trapped in the past.
4. The Loop Closes
Overwhelmed with panic, he fled back toward the forest to figure out how to return to his timeline. When he reached the clearing where the strange event had happened earlier, everything looked normal. But before he could process his surroundings, a sudden blow struck him from behind, plunging him into darkness.
When he finally regained consciousness, he woke up in a strange, unfamiliar area, captured by the beings with glowing eyes.
As he peeked out from his confinement, he heard the familiar sound of his own truck approaching. Peering through the trees, he saw his past self—the man he used to be—arriving, and noticing someone hiding behind a tree.
By the light shining from his captors' eyes, the trapped man finally saw the truth clearly. He looked at his own face staring back at him from the past. He was the one who had been hiding behind the tree all along.
It was a closed time loop. He understood now: the trees growing within a certain radius of this mystical forest were ancient time-travel anomalies, connected to different planets or dimensions. Anyone who stepped inside their radius was doomed to be caught forever in an endless cycle of time.
