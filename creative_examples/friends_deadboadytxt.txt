
[REGISTER: feel-good comedy / suspense / dark humor / Telugu-English (Tenglish)]
[TEACHES: situational comedy, physical comedy, deadpan landlord vs. panicking friends, 
          suspense through near-misses, Tenglish slang, 
          escalation of absurdity, humour through character contradiction]
          
**EXT. RENTED VILLA - PORTICO - NIGHT**

A rusted iron gate *CREAKS* loudly in the silence. 

BUNNY (24) and RITHVIK (24) are dragging the LIMP, UNCONSCIOUS BODY of a burly guy between them. The guy's sneakers leave twin skid marks in the gravel. 

ABHI (23) is trailing behind, frantically trying to wipe sweat off his face with a crumpled handkerchief.

BUNNY
(whispering furiously)
Rey Chetan! Gate sound vachindi ra! Evado vasthunnadu!

RITHVIK
Lopaliki laagu ra vadini! Rey Chetan, kaallu pattuko mundhu!

ABHI
(frozen, hyperventilating)
Gunde kottukoledhu mowa vadiki... assam aypoyadu vadu... manam jail ki pothunam...

Before they can react, a heavy flashlight beam blinds them.

VENKAT RAO (58)—the landlord, wearing a checked lungi and half-sleeve banyan, holding a steel flask and a torch—stands at the portico steps. He squints through his thick glasses.

VENKAT RAO
Evaru akkada? Rey Bunny? Enti ee racha ee time lo?

Bunny and Rithvik instantly stiffen. With an unnatural surge of panic, they hoist the limp man up between them, pinning him upright against their shoulders. 

The limp guy’s head instantly flops backward like a broken doll. 

Bunny quickly slips his hand behind the guy’s neck, cupping his skull and forcing his face straight ahead.

BUNNY
(voice an octave too high)
Uncle! Namaste uncle! Meeru inka padukoledha?

VENKAT RAO
(pointing the torch at them)
Padukoniye range lo unnaara meeru? Gate ala dhabba dhabba laadinchi theesthunte nidra ela vasthadi? Evaru vadu?

RITHVIK
(grunting under the weight)
Vadu... ma friend uncle. College dosth. Ee roje Hyderabad vachadu. 

VENKAT RAO
Mari kaallu nelaki thagalatledhu enti vadiki? Galloki leparu?

BUNNY
(chuckling awkwardly)
Adhi... Uncle, chinna celebration jarigindi le bayata. Konchem... ekuva theesesukunnadu. Full tight aypoyadu uncle.

VENKAT RAO
(shaking his head, clicking tongue)
Thagadam. Ee generation motham ee daridranike tagaladindi. Okka chukka thagithe saripodha? Bottle motham kadupuloki dimpala?

Rao starts walking down the steps toward them. 

Bunny's fingers are digging deep into the limp man's collar. The dead weight is pulling them down inch by inch. 

RITHVIK
(teeth clenched, whispering)
Rey Bunny... na bhujam virigipothundi ra...

VENKAT RAO
(stopping three feet away)
Choodandi, mee age lo unnapudu nenu kuda friends tho thirigaanu. Kaani limit undedi. Liver lu em aypothunnayo thelusa meeku? Oka peg paddaka brain control undali. Control lekapothe manishi ki, pashuvuki theda enti?

Rao turns slightly, gesturing toward the garden to demonstrate his point.

VENKAT RAO (CONT'D)
Naaku thelsina oka abbayi unde vadu. Same ilane beer lu, whiskey lu kurchuni direct ga thaagi—

The moment Rao looks away, the limp body goes slack. His legs slide forward. 

Rithvik loses his grip. The body starts sliding down Bunny’s chest like melting butter. 

BUNNY
(eyes bulging, smiling at Rao)
Avunu uncle... absolutely right uncle...

Bunny desperately grabs the dead guy by the belt loops, yanking him back up. 

Abhi, standing behind, panics and shoves his own forehead against the dead guy's back to keep him propped upright.

VENKAT RAO
(turning back suddenly)
Em chesthunnaru akkada?

Instantly, Rithvik violently pushes the dead guy's head down toward the concrete flower pot near the pillar.

RITHVIK
Uncle! Vaddhu uncle! Daggarki raakandi! Veediki vomiting start ayyindi! 

BUNNY
Haan uncle! Thupuk ani bayataki kakkuthunnadu! Mee lungi meeda padthadi!

Abhi immediately drops down behind the flower pot and makes loud, violent gagging noises.

ABHI
*(from the shadows)*
BLEEEGGHH! Oooorrkkk!

Rithvik repeatedly pats the dead guy’s back with aggressive sympathy.

RITHVIK
Kakkara... kakkai... manchiga bayataki pampiyyi...

Venkat Rao jumps back three feet, visibly disgusted, pulling his lungi up by his knees.

VENKAT RAO
Chi chi chi! Thu! Naa chetla pot la meeda kakkuthunnada vadu? 

BUNNY
Chala sorry uncle, vadiki sudden ga motion sickness kuda vachindi. Memu lopaliki theskeltham lendi.

Venkat Rao shines the torch on the guy’s dangling left arm. The skin is pale and completely limp.

Rao hesitates. He steps forward cautiously, reaches out, and grabs the guy's forearm.

BUNNY
(heart in his throat)
Uncle...?

VENKAT RAO
(rubbing the guy's arm briskly)
Rey! Cheyyi antha ice la aypoyindi ra. Ice cold aypoyindi body motham. Choodu, nenu cheppedhi vinte kada meeru? BP padipoyindi vadiki. Excessive alcohol valla de-hydration vachindi. 

Rao fumbles into his banyan pocket and pulls out a silver strip of tablets. He pops two out into his palm.

VENKAT RAO (CONT'D)
Idhigo. Rendu Pantop tablets unnay. Gastric pressure tagguthe koddiga relief vasthadi. Nootlo petti manchi neellu thaginchi, fan kinda padukobettandi. 

Rao holds out the tablets. Bunny, hands occupied holding the corpse's collar, nudges Rithvik with his elbow. 

Rithvik slowly lets go of the dead guy's shoulder with one trembling hand to take the pills. 

RITHVIK
Thanks uncle. Manchi manishi meeru.

VENKAT RAO
Manchi manishi kabatte chepthunna. Ikkada evadiki evadu padadhu ee kalam lo. Aa vomit meeda water kottandi tharvatha. Smell vasthe compaint chestha.

BUNNY
Pakka uncle! Motham clean chesestham!

Venkat Rao turns around, shaking his head in disappointment, and starts walking toward the gate.

VENKAT RAO
(muttering to himself)
Dharidrulu... jeevithalu nasanam cheskuntunnaru...

The moment Rao steps out of the portico and the gate *CLICKS* shut—

Bunny and Rithvik immediately let go. 

*THUD.* 

The body drops flat onto the cold tiles, face-first.

Silence for two seconds. Only the panting breath of the three friends.

Abhi crawls out from behind the flower pot, wiping his mouth, looking at the two pills in Rithvik’s trembling hand.

ABHI
(voice shaking, near tears)
Vadu... chachipoyadu kada ra? Ippudu ee tablet ni vadiki ekkada nunchi thaginchali?
