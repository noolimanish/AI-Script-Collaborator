INT. WINE SHOP — EVENING
A small, cramped wine shop. Bottles stacked behind a steel mesh counter. A tube light buzzes. AUNTY (40s–50s) sits on a plastic stool, sorting coins.

The door swings open. DRUNK GUY (30s) stumbles in. He's swaying. Smiling. Too happy.

DRUNK GUY

Namaste aunty. Ela unnaru? Antha manchide na?

Aunty doesn't look up.

AUNTY

Avuna. Ee madya baga girak vastundi ani vinna.

DRUNK GUY

(grinning)
Endi? Malla entha vastunay endi paysal?

AUNTY

(finally looking up)
Neeku enduku ra paysal? Unnene mandu.

DRUNK GUY

(raising a hand)
Uko aunty. Nuvu gamathu matladuthunav. Nenu mandu apesi mastu rpjulu aytundi.

AUNTY

Malla itu enduku vachinav?

DRUNK GUY

Ago atla antav. Ravodu aa endi? Edo muchata pedam ani vachina. Nuvu gitla antava? Mana bandam inte na? Chi.

AUNTY

Ha. Mana bandam ne katha anatha. Adi kattu mundu.

DRUNK GUY

Ha kadtham. Ediki potha? Ee urlane sastha kada. Ediki urkathale kadha.

AUNTY

Sare. Kani auty — aa mila mila marustundi. Aa akupacha dhi. Bottlu. Eppudu chudale. Em brand adi? Kothadi aa?

AUNTY

(deadpan)
Neeku enduku ra? Nuvu apesinav ga.

DRUNK GUY

Ee uthigane adigina. Edo koshaga, kallaki kodtunte adigina.

AUNTY

(sighs)
Chal. Paysal katti po. Badkow. Ninu namma.

DRUNK GUY

(leaning on the counter)
Ne amma paysal paysal ediki potha? Ne kadtha. Vadi tho shaa kadta.

Aunty stares at him. Long. Hard.

Drunk guy stares back. Smiling. Swaying.

Aunty picks up a bottle. Not the akupacha one. A cheap one. Slides it across the counter.

AUNTY

Idi teesko. Free. Kani inka itu rvaddu.

Drunk guy grabs the bottle. Hugs it.

DRUNK GUY

Aunty. Nuvu devi. Nuvu lakshmi. Nuvu—

AUNTY

(pointing to the door)
Po.

He stumbles out. Door swings shut.

Aunty shakes her head. Goes back to sorting coins.

FADE OUT.
