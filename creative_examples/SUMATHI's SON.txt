[REGISTER: quiet realism / family drama / subtext]
[TEACHES: boundary enforcement through physical action, subtext over dialogue, 
          protective maternal instinct, quiet resolution]

**INT. SUMATHI’S HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - EVENING**

A cramped hall in an old Malkajgiri house. The low drone of a pedestal fan oscillates against the peeled green wall paint.

TEJA (8) sits on the carpet, transfixed by a glossy red remote-control car. The headlights flash blue and red across the floor tiles.

On the sofa above him sits VENKAT (42). Safari suit, a thick gold ring on his pinky finger, smelling of Mysore Sandal soap and pan masala. He watches the boy with an easy, lingering smile.

VENKAT
Speed baagunda Teja?

TEJA
(eyes wide, gripping the plastic remote)
Chala fast pothundi Babanji! Road meedha kooda run avthada?

VENKAT
Road meedhane running. Kinda mana bullet bike meedha petti, key niku istha. Padha, kindaki veldam.

Teja scrambles up, ready to sprint out.

SUMATHI (34) steps out from the kitchen carrying a steel tumbler of lukewarm tea. Her hair is loosely pinned, saree pallu tucked into her waist, her forearms dusted with dry flour.

She stops at the doorway. Her eyes flick from the red toy to Venkat’s hand resting casually near Teja’s shoulder.

SUMATHI
Chai theeskondi bava.

Venkat doesn’t reach for the tea immediately. He leans forward, resting his elbows on his knees.

VENKAT
Maradhalu... Teja ni bayataki theskelthunna. Just aa Chilkalguda bridge daaka velli, ice cream thipinchi thestha. Araganta.

TEJA
(jumping)
Amma, bullet meedha anta! Please nenu potha!

Sumathi places the tea tumbler on the teapoy with a soft, deliberate click. She does not look at Venkat.

SUMATHI
Velladu.

Venkat chuckles, shaking his head.

VENKAT
Enduku maradhalu? Choodu, vadu entha excited unnaado. Pillalani illallo petti lock chesthe em ఎదుగుతారు? Manam unnam ga chuskodaniki.

SUMATHI
(calm, flat)
Homework inka poorthi kaaledhu. Repu unit test undi.

TEJA
Amma! Test Monday kada! Repu Sunday!

Sumathi looks directly at Teja. Her gaze is steady, devoid of anger, but completely unmovable.

SUMATHI
Teja. Bag theesi maths table meedha pettu. Ippude vasthunna.

TEJA
(whining, stomping his foot)
Prathi saari anthe nuvvu! Babanji pilusthunte enduku apthav? Roju intlone kurchovala nenu?

Venkat smiles, stretching his arm out toward the boy’s head.

VENKAT
Parledhu le ra, valla amma ki bayam ekkuva. Chinnapudu mee nanna kuda itlane bayapadevadu maa nanna ante. Nene kurchobetti anni nerpincha. (looks up at Sumathi) Pilladiki eppudo okappudu teliyali kada maradhalu, bayata prapancham ela untado? Nenu daggara undi chuskunta kada... vere evaro kaadhu ga.

Sumathi steps in smoothly, angling her body directly between Venkat’s outstretched hand and Teja. The reach is broken.

SUMATHI
Maa prapancham intlo varake baava. Bayataki vachi nerchukovalsina avasaram vadiki inka raledhu.

Venkat’s smile stiffens at the edges. His fingers curl back slowly.

VENKAT
Sare. Nee ishtam. Kani maree antha tight ga petti penchaku. Tharvatha vadike chethilo nunchi jaari poyina feeling vasthadi.

He picks up the tea tumbler, takes a small sip, and sets it back down.

VENKAT (CONT'D)
Nenu vastha mari. Thammudu vachaka cheppu, shop daggara wait chesthunta ani.

Venkat picks up his helmet from the teapoy, glances at Teja one last time, and walks toward the main door.

The door clicks shut behind him.

Teja stands by the wall, tears prickling in his eyes, furiously holding the plastic remote.

TEJA
Nuvvu asalu manchidanivi kaavu Amma. Epudu intlane untav, nannu kooda ekkadiki ponivvavu. Nenu Babanji tho matladanu inka, neetho kooda matladanu!

Teja throws the remote on the carpet and storms into the bedroom, slamming the door halfway shut.

Sumathi stands in the center of the quiet hall. The red toy car on the floor continues to flash its tiny blue and red LEDs in the dimming twilight.

She walks over. She picks up the toy car, switches off the small black slider at the bottom, and sets it on the shelf, out of reach.

**INT. BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS**

The room is dark except for the streetlight filtering through the rusted window grill.

Teja is curled up sideways on the narrow cot, his back turned to the door, staring bitterly at the wall, sniffing quietly.

The mattress dips slightly as Sumathi sits on the edge beside him.

Teja immediately stiffens, pulling his knees closer to his chest to shut her out.

Sumathi doesn't speak. She doesn't explain the door she just closed. She doesn't tell him why that car was bought, or what kind of silence lives in houses where men like Venkat are allowed to be generous.

She just sits there.

Slowly, she takes the damp corner of her cotton saree pallu. She reaches over and gently pulls his small, clenched right hand into hers.

Teja resists for a second, then lets his fingers go limp with stubborn resentment.

With slow, rhythmic strokes, Sumathi quietly wipes away the sticky sweet syrup left on his small fingers from the laddu Venkat had handed him at the door—rubbing his skin completely clean, over and over, until nothing of the man remains on the boy.
