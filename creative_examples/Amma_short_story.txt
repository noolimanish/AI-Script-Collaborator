SCENE 1 — INT. HOUSE, HALL — NIGHT (PRESENT DAY)
Darkness. The sound of heavy breathing. A single bulb flickers.

RAGHUNATH (40s, police SI uniform, now torn and bloodied) is on the floor. He is dragging himself backward. His face is white with fear. Blood trails behind him.

SAROJINI (30s, worn saree, bruised face) stands frozen in a corner. Her eyes are wide. Her hands tremble. She is too scared to move.

A pair of HANDS enter the frame — covered in blood. One hand grips a KNIFE. The blood drips from the blade onto the floor.

The hands belong to NITHIN. We do not see his face yet.

NITHIN (O.S., screaming)

AAAAAA...... Endukuuu..... Endukuuuu.... Itlaa enduku chesinavvvvvv....... AAAAAAaaaaaa.....

The hands rise. And then — brutally — the knife comes down into Raghunath's body. Again. Again. Again. Like a madman. Like a psycho.

Sarojini watches. Her mouth opens to scream — but no sound comes. She FAINTS, collapsing to the floor.

Nithin's hands are shivering. Adrenaline. Rage. Trauma. Everything at once.

The knife slips from his grip. Falls.

CUT TO BLACK.

SCENE 2 — EXT. HOUSE, OUTSIDE — NIGHT
Blue police lights flash against the walls. A crowd has gathered. Whispering. Pointing.

POLICE VAN. Two constables lead NITHIN out — his hands cuffed behind his back.

The camera starts from BEHIND him — his hair, matted with blood. His shoulders. His torn shirt. Then slowly — his back. His neck.

He stops. Slowly TURNS BACK.

We see his face for the first time. Blood on his cheeks. Eyes — empty, yet calm.

He looks at his mother — SAROJINI — sitting far away on the ground, silent, scared, confused.

NITHIN

Jagratha amma. Sorry amma. Ilantivolu bathukodu amma. Ayna nenu ila avvadiniki karnam veedee.

The crowd murmurs. Some take videos. Some cry.

Suddenly — Nithin's voice rises. Loud. Angry. Broken.

NITHIN

Andaru vinnadi chusaru ga emindoo.... AAa pillalni pechadam rakapothe kanakandi anthe kani kanni torture cheyakandi.

His voice cracks. Tears mix with the blood on his face.

NITHIN (crying)

Chinnapudu nunchi naku telusu nenu em em chusano ela pergano ani. Please, please, me andariki dandam pedtha — nanu chusi anna, me pillani ila kakuda chuskondi.

Nenu em psycho ni kadu. Naku badhalu, kopam ani lopal lopalaaa... ala ala nindi visham la maripoyindi. Na valla kaledu. Inka nenu chudalenu. Anduke eee chetha na koduku ni champese. Ippudu ma amma ela untundo naku telidu kani, aa narkam lonunchi ayte baytaki vastadi...

He looks at his mother. Emotion floods his face.

Sarojini — far away — suddenly breaks. Screams.

SAROJINI

Teskelipondi saar! Vedini dayachesi teskapondi! Na nunchi duramga teskellandi saar! Veedu pichodu aypoyadu sir! Naaku veedu odhu! Na jeevitham lo chudanu vidini!

Nithin hears this. And — surprisingly — he SMILES. A smile of pain. Of understanding.

He is happy. Because he thinks his mother is finally free. Free from pain. Free to live however she wants.

But he doesn't understand — her life, her everything, was because of HIM. To watch his success. To shut every mouth in society. To prove his father wrong.

Everything is disturbed now. Everything is different.

Nithin constantly watches his mother. She smiles slowly.

The scene FADES.

SCENE 3 — INT. THEATER — 2002 — NIGHT
TITLE CARD: 2002.

A cinema hall. The film JAYAM is playing on screen. The audience laughs and cheers.

In the seats — RAGHUNATH (younger, arrogant, police SI) and SAROJINI (9 months pregnant, uncomfortable).

Sarojini shifts. Winces. Holds her belly.

SAROJINI

Evandi, naku chala pain aytundi. Naku artham aytale. Veldam padha...

RAGHUNATH (eyes on screen)

Aagu nenu movie chustuna. Nuve po. Bayta auto matladikoni.

Deeni movie teskaravam ee chala kastam ga undi. Eedo inspector tesakara free tickets ista ante techina. Ippude ravala deeniki pains.

He waves his hand — irritated. Calls out to his constable.

RAGHUNATH

Rey Vamsi! Itu ra. Madam ki pains vastunay anta. Edina auto appi ekkiyu. Mana deggarla unna hospital pothadi...

VAMSI (constable, nervous)

Saar, adhi mere... ante mere velthe madam kuda dhairyam ga untadi kadha sir...

RAGHUNATH

Enti? Edo ne pellam annatu tega feel avuthunav. Cheptuna kadha — chepindi matrame chey. Ledha ante nuve poi drop chesi hospital bill kuda kattu mala...

VAMSI

Ayyo ledu sir. Nenu auto ne matladutha...

Vamsi rushes. Sarojini — suffering — calls after him.

SAROJINI

Vamsi, ma ayna ekkada? Tondara ramanu...

VAMSI

Amma, nuvu hospital vellu. Sir bike venake vastuntadu...

Sarojini — alone, in pain — pulls out her phone. Dials. Calls her sisters.

SCENE 4 — INT. HOSPITAL, LABOR ROOM — NIGHT
Sarojini lies on the bed. Sweating. Crying. Around her — her sisters. PADMA (elder), and others.

A baby's cry fills the room.

PADMA (overjoyed)

Saru! Neeku babu puttinde! Abba, chala mudu ga unnadu! Inka aa rakshashudu nunchi ninu veede rakshistadu!

Sarojini — exhausted but overwhelmed — holds the baby. Tears of happiness. Everyone around her shows love. Care. Warmth.

PADMA

Agu, ee me ayna cheptha. Ippatiki ayna vastadu emo... Ayna first abbaayi putthe abbadam cheppali anta thandri ki...

She picks up the landline. Dials Raghunath.

PADMA

Hello Raghunath? Neeku ammai puttindi. Vaste chudochu...

CLICK. The line goes dead. The sound of the receiver being SLAMMED.

Padma looks at the phone. Confused. Hurt.

SCENE 5 — INT. HOSPITAL ROOM — NEXT DAY
Raghunath enters. He doesn't look at the baby. He doesn't smile. He doesn't show love.

RAGHUNATH

Ammai ani enduku cheparu asalu? Vinangane vadla ginja esi sampedam anukuna...

PADMA

Abbai putthe abbadam chepali atla. Anduke chepinam. Neeku koduke puttindu thi.

RAGHUNATH

Aa manchidhi. Naku pani undi. Velli osta.

He leaves. Doesn't look back. Doesn't hold the child. Doesn't ask about Sarojini.

Sarojini watches him go. Her eyes — hurt. But she holds her baby tighter.

SCENE 6 — INT. HOUSE — DAYS LATER — THE NAMING
Sarojini sits with the baby. She's humming. Happy.

SAROJINI

Na koduku peru Nithin. Nithin... Nithin... Ullu uluulu...

The baby LAUGHS. Delighted. He reaches out. Holds her hand tight.

And then — slowly — he murmurs.

BABY

Mmaaa...

Sarojini freezes. Shocked. Overjoyed.

SAROJINI

Ayyo! Amma annadu! Amma annadu!

She hugs him. Cries. Laughs. She thinks he's too intelligent. She pours all her love, all her expectations into him.

She teaches him everything. Every day. She wants to make him a good man. And he — even as a baby — is caring to her. Reaches for her. Smiles at her.

For a while — they are happy.

SCENE 7 — INT. HOUSE — NIGHT — THE FIRST TRAUMA
The baby — now a few months old — lies in his cradle. Sarojini is in the kitchen.

Raghunath enters. Drunk. Angry about something small.

He looks at the food. Tastes it. Spits.

RAGHUNATH

Chi! Em taste ra idi?

He grabs the bowl of curry — and THROWS it at Sarojini's head.

The bowl strikes her forehead. Cracks. Curry and blood mix, running down her face.

She screams. Falls to the floor.

The BABY — in his cradle — WATCHES. His eyes go wide. His tiny body trembles.

He CRIES. Loud. Piercing.

And he doesn't stop. Not that night. Not the next. He cries almost 2 DAYS continuously.

Sarojini, bleeding, terrified, holds him. Rocks him. Nothing works. He just cries.

From that day — the baby STOPS smiling. STOPS crying. Something inside him has broken.

SCENE 8 — INT. CLINIC — DAY
Sarojini sits with the baby. The doctor examines him.

DOCTOR

Em ayyindi amma? Babu edustunadu ani cheparu?

SAROJINI

Em ledu sir. Emana ayyi undochu...

She has no money. She took money from somewhere hidden in the house. She's scared.

The doctor says something. We don't hear the words. But Sarojini nods. Holds her baby tighter.

SCENE 9 — INT. HOUSE — NIGHT — THE CONFRONTATION
Raghunath finds out she went to the doctor.

He grabs her by the hair. Slaps her. Hits her. Again. Again.

RAGHUNATH

Veedu na koduku ee kadhu. Evadothoni kannavo emo naku telidu. Veddu adangi vadu eppud amma amma ani eduskunta untadu. Chi! Naku ilanti valu dorkinaru endi ne amma...

Neeku anna mana pani manshi baguntadi kadha ne? Dantho kanna aypovu? Koncham tellaga, andamga, dayram ga unnodu pudthunde...

Sarojini — broken, bleeding — runs to the phone. Calls her father.

SCENE 10 — INT. HOUSE — NIGHT — THE PHONE CALL
Sarojini's hands shake as she dials. The baby — Nithin — lies nearby. Watching with hollow eyes.

SAROJINI (into phone)

Abbha, nenu uru vachesta. Inkkada naku aytale. Kotti kotti samputunaru. Nuvuemo katnam ista ani iyyale. Thamudu emo bandi ipista ani ippiyale. Ivvitiki nanu samputunadu. Kani na koduku kuda edo aytuna abbha. Ida unte, nenu uru vachesta. Naku eedu vaddu.

On the other end — KANKARAO (her father, 50s, cold, society-fearing).

KANKARAO

Saru, nuvu adne undali. Vachesta etla? Andaru em anukuntaru? Pilla intiki vachesindi ante? Ayna em sakulu ee ivi? Anni manchigne chestadu kadha. Illu unde, tindi unde, anni unnye kada amma. Idaki vachi na izzat teyaku amma.

Oka debba esta vachestava endi? Mogudu annaku kodtadu, tidtadu. Nuvve artham cheskovali. Neeku inka koduku unnadu. Vaniki kudu ne mogudu ee petalli paysal evaru pettaru. Arthamaytundhaaa? Adne undu. Urike sakulu eskoni vachayaku intiki... Nenu eto atta estam le katam...

CLICK. The phone goes dead.

Sarojini stands there. Frozen.

The words hit her like blows. She thought her loving father would understand. But he reacted the same way.

She looks at the baby. Then at the knife in the kitchen.

She walks toward it.

SCENE 11 — INT. HOUSE — LATER THAT NIGHT
Sarojini sits on the floor. The knife beside her. Her eyes are dead.

She has decided. She will die. She doesn't want to live anymore.

She has attempted this before — several times, before her pregnancy. Each time — saved in a hospital.

She reaches for the knife.

And then — from the other room — a sound.

NITHIN is CRYING. Not a normal cry. A desperate, piercing, heartbroken cry. Louder than he has ever cried.

Sarojini freezes. The knife slips from her hand.

She runs to him. Picks him up.

SAROJINI

Nittuu nana, ikkada unna ra. Vachesna. Ammani vachesna chudu. Edvaku nana. Pallu kavali aa? Ista tesko. Ingo bommalu aduko... aduko...

The baby stops crying. Looks at her. Holds her finger. And — slowly — SMILES. For the first time since that night.

Sarojini breaks down. But this time — not in despair.

She decides. She will NOT die.

She will live for her child. She will make him efficient in everything. She will see her son in a strong, mature position in this world.

That will be her only win — over society, over her husband, over everyone.

She holds Nithin close. Rocks him.

SAROJINI (whispering)

Nee kosame ra. Nee kosame.

FADE TO BLACK.

SCENE 1 — INT. HOUSE — MORNING
Small kitchen. Steam rising. SAROJINI (30s, tired eyes, worn saree) is packing lunch. NITHIN (7–8, school uniform, small school bag) sits at the table.

NITHIN

Amma, em chestunav food eroju?

SAROJINI

Em undi ra. Pappu annam.

NITHIN

Ne amma eppudu pappu ee naa? Na frnd ayte eppudu chicken, mutton ani techukuntunaru. Nuvu emo pappu sambar ivve naa. Only Sunday na amma non-veg?

SAROJINI (smiles weakly)

Ne ayyani aduko ra. Temani non-veg chesipedta. Na chetilo emundi?

Nithin picks up his socks — torn at the heel.

NITHIN

Amma, na socks chinigipoyay. Kothavi kavali. Daddy ni adigi ippiyu. Navutunaru amma schoollo...

SAROJINI

Sare ra. Adugutha le.

She kisses his head. He leaves.

SCENE 2 — INT. HOUSE — AFTERNOON
RAGHUNATH (40s, police SI uniform, arrogant) enters. Throws his cap on the table.

RAGHUNATH

Em chesav eroju? Food petu. Akali aytundi.

SAROJINI

Ha testuna. Pappu chesa eroju.

Raghunath looks at the plate. Without a word — he throws it at her. Hot sambar splashes on her hand. Skin burns.

SAROJINI (shouting)

Nachakapothe tinaku! Endi padeyatam? Neekosam cheyandiniki lenu nenu. Nenu chesindi tinu. Lekpothe bayta poi techukoni tinu!

Raghunath grabs his belt. Hits her. Again. Again.

RAGHUNATH

Endi ee matalu lestunay? Nuvu nanu ane antha aynava? Na paysal ee ivi. Muskoni naku manchivi ondale. Nee koduku ki kadhu nenu paysal ichedi. Nakosam.

SAROJINI (crying)

Chi. Chinapiladu medha padi edustav endi eppudu? Vadu nee koduku kuda. Okasari anna manchiga matladu. Vadi kosame manam em chesina...

RAGHUNATH

Chi. Vadu na koduku ee kadhu. Vaniki tindi petti intlo unchukodam ee ekkuva.

The door opens. NITHIN enters — school finished. He freezes. Sees everything.

SAROJINI (quickly)

Vaniki socks levu. Bag chingipoindi. School veltunadu. Vadu friends untaru. Navutunaru anta chusi. Avvi ippiyali. Nuvu first avvi chusko. Nanu kottadam kadu.

RAGHUNATH

Enti ee noru lestundi? Nenu petanu ee na paysal. Atlane po manu. Nuvu me ayya etla poinaru? Gudshala undetolu meru. Mee enduku ee luxury? Meru eppudu na kalakinda ne undali.

Chepu vaniki — nuvu ekkadina padukoni testa, anni konista ani. Neeku ivvi ani alvate ga. Asalu vadu na koduku ani proof endi ee asalu?

NITHIN stands outside the door. Listening. Not understanding every word — but feeling every word. He doesn't enter. Out of fear.

RAGHUNATH

Koduku chusi aguthalevu aa nuvu? Vadu gitla naku virudamga tayaru aynadu anuko — sampesta vanii.

Sarojini snaps. Throws a spoon at him.

SAROJINI

Thu ne abba! Nuvu thandri ve na ra asalu? Chinna pillodni patukoni eni matlau ela annabudhi aytundi ra neeku? Vadu pedha aynaka neeku yamudu avvaka pothe chudu ra. Purugulu pati pothav. Chii...

She cries.

RAGHUNATH

Ne amma broker munda. Em matladtunave? Nanu kodsthavaaa?

He throws the stool beside her. It hits her forehead. Blood.

NITHIN — hearing the crash — runs inside. Sees his mother bleeding.

NITHIN (crying, holding her)

Amma! Em ayyindi amma? Blood vastundi ammaa! Naku socks bag inka emi vadu amma. Nenu pedha aynaka konukunta avii. Ippudu emi vadu. Daddy, naku amma ni kottaku!

Raghunath grabs Nithin. Throws him aside. Nithin hits the wall — hard. His head strikes the corner. He doesn't even realise he's bleeding.

Nithin — terrified — runs upstairs.

SCENE 3 — INT. UPSTAIRS ROOM — CONTINUOUS
Nithin bangs on the door. MAMA (Sarojini's younger brother, 20s) opens.

NITHIN (panicking)

Mama! Mama! Kindaki ra! Amma ki full blood vastundi! Daddy kodtunadu amma ni!

MAMA

Neeku emindi ra? Blood vastundi! Padha kindaki. Vastunaa nenu.

Mama rushes down. Sees Sarojini bleeding. Sees Raghunath.

MAMA

Bava, enduku atla akka ni antla koduthunav? Chudu chinna pillodini kodthe thala pagilindi. Emaina yate etla?

RAGHUNATH

Aa pedhamanshi vachinava? Kodadam kadhu. Sampestha dini eroju. Vadu evadu? Falthu gadu. Naku koduku kadu adu.

MAMA

First akka itu hospital podam. Viniki chudu etla takindoo. Ayna pilladnini pettukoni nuvu kuda endi? Akka gammuna undochu kadha?

Sarojini is sobbing.

SAROJINI

Auyyoo Nithuu... em indi ra thala ki... Chi kukka. Em bathuku ra needi. Veedini mkodthava? Nee abba nne savu veni chethilone vestadu ra devudu...

She runs to the pooja room.

SAROJINI (praying, broken)

Devuduaaa... Nuvu nizamgane unte eee lanj... kodukuni chavu... Na koduku chetilone undali devudaaaaa...

MAMA

Akka, em matladuthunav? Chinapillodini patukoni... hospital podam pa. Jaldi.

RAGHUNATH

Aggguuuu! Ediki teskapothunav? Ippudu teskapo. Kani valani mala intiki pampodu. Nuvu chesko vala karchulu. Atla ayte teskapoo.

MAMA

Endi bava atlantav? Manam kurchoni tarvatha matladudam. Kani ippudu raktham karuthundi. Emaina ayte kastam mala.

RAGHUNATH

Em aytadi? Sastaru iddaru. Rey nenu police ra. Em cheyali, etla cheyali naku telusu. Nuvu dengay idikelli. First na katam kattu manu ne aayani. Tarvatha matladu. Matalu chal.

He shuts the door in Mama's face.

Mama cries. Leaves in pain. Later — moves out of the building entirely. Stays far away from his sister.

SCENE 4 — EXT. HOSPITAL — NIGHT
Doctor examines Nithin. A deep cut on the back of his head. He shivers. Eyes blank.

DOCTOR

Em ayyindi amma? Babu pedha deep cut ayyindi. Ee age look chuskokapothe kastam pillalni. Ippudu vadi emaina avvachu. Brain ki koncham touch ayyindi. Chudu — ela shiver aytundo. Edo bayamkaramga chusinatu.

SAROJINI

Em ledu sir. Adukunta kinda padinadu.

DOCTOR

Ippatiki ayte em problem ledu. Stitches esina, medicines icha. Eppudina continuous headaches kani, sudden ga different behave chesina nanu kalvandi. And more over — naku artham aytundi me situation. Don't do anything in front of child which scares or traumatises him. Me manchike cheptuna.

SAROJINI

Thanks doctor sir. Veluthamm memu inka.

SCENE 5 — INT. HOUSE — DAYS LATER — MONTAGE
The same thing. Every day. Every night.

Raghunath hitting Sarojini.

Nithin watching.

Nithin not crying.

Nithin not laughing.

He has stopped both since the hospital.

CUT TO:

Nithin, now 12 (6th class), comes home from football. Sweating. Happy — for once.

NITHIN

Amma! Vachesaa! Nenu em chesa telusa eroju? Nenu football adina. And I scored 2 goals. Team ki na valle gelchindi annaru andaru. Telusaa?

Sarojini — in the balcony, washing clothes. Silent. Staring at something in her hand.

Nithin walks closer.

NITHIN

Emindi amma? Em matladthalevu asalu?

He sees — she's crying. Looking at something.

NITHIN

Amma, enti avvi? Enduku avvi chusi edustunav? Nuvu edvaku amma.

She's holding CONDOMS. Found in Raghunath's pocket. She quickly hides them.

SAROJINI

Kadu nana. Aavi ballons. Naku kalalo surf padindi. Andulo water vastunay...

NITHIN

Ballons aa? Daddy techadu aa? Enduku? Evari birthday eroju?

SAROJINI (voice breaking)

Nee birthday undi kadha nana. Next month. Dani kosam.

She cries harder. Ashamed.

NITHIN

Naku em odu aa ballons. Padesay.

SAROJINI

Sare nana. Nuvu elli aduko.

SCENE 6 — INT. HOUSE — NIGHT
Sarojini waits. Late. Raghunath enters — drunk, arrogant.

SAROJINI

Ivvi enti?

RAGHUNATH

Nee koduku lantiodni inkokadni rakunda apaniki. Condoms antaru vetini.

SAROJINI

Adhi naku telusu. Nee pocketlo enduku unnay?

SLAP.

RAGHUNATH

Na istam ee. Nenu pandukuna bayta andamina ammailatho. Intlo needlanti leeki mokam chuste naku mood ravatle. Anduke bayta vethukuna.

Ayna neeku nenu enduku chepali ee? Kavali ante nuvu poi panduko. Nee koduki paysal collection ki petuko. Po.

SAROJINI

Thu nee bathuku. Intha kamam gadini pellicheskoni anubavistuna. Edana savu ra poi nuvu.

Raghunath hits her hard. She falls. Hits her head on the bed. Bleeds from forehead.

RAGHUNATH

Nuvu nee koduku ellipondi. Vanni saduvupistuna nenu ee nuvu kadhu. Chetha munda.

NITHIN wakes up. Comes to the hall. Watches. Says nothing. Feels nothing. Or — feels everything, and shows nothing.

His father notices him.

RAGHUNATH

Endi ra broker kodka? Kopam ga chustunav? Medalu ersesta. Itu ra. Nee marks enni ra?

Nithin — under pressure, under trauma — can't study. Can't get marks. His father uses it.

Raghunath drags him to a room. Closes the door. Belt.

We hear the belt. We hear nothing from Nithin.

Raghunath opens the door. Nithin hasn't cried. Hasn't flinched.

RAGHUNATH

Neeku entha pogaru ra? Inkodadiki putinaodaa? Sampesta ninu. Inkosari ilanti esalu esinav anuko.

SCENE 7 — INT./EXT. HOUSE — NIGHT — MONTAGE
Nithin's trauma deepens.

He sleeps alone.

Wakes suddenly at midnight.

Goes outside. Plays with street dogs. Talks to them for hours.

The dogs are the only ones who don't hurt him.

No friends at school.

No one asks him what happened.

CUT TO:

School. A boy says "nee amma..." casually. Nithin snaps. Attacks him brutally.

NITHIN

Arey nee amma antava nanu? Ninu sampesta ra eroju! Aypoynav nuvu!

Teachers pull him off. Principal calls Sarojini.

SCENE 8 — INT. HOUSE — EVENING
Sarojini confronts Nithin.

SAROJINI

Rey, enduku ra itla aynav nuvu? Nuvu me ayya leka aytunava endi? Chepu. Ninu ila penchaleee?

Nithin explodes. Throws things around the house. Shouts like a madman. His body burns. His head is hot.

Sarojini — terrified — takes him to the family doctor.

SCENE 9 — INT. CLINIC — NIGHT
DOCTOR

Nenu chapanu kadha amma — vadi mundu elantivi em cheyodu ani. Vadu oka immense trauma lo unnadu ippudu. Vadini apali ante chala sessions kavali. Vadu ilane unte pichodu ayi, sudden ga adrenaline rush ayyi heat kuda agipovachu. Me manchi ke cheptuna.

SAROJINI (crying)

Ayyo doctor, ala kadu. Meru emana cheyandi. Nenu ista dabbulu. Treatment cheyandi doctor. Vadu chala manchodu doctor. Ila ela ayyado teliyatle...

SCENE 10 — INT. HOUSE — NIGHT
Sarojini talks to Raghunath.

SAROJINI

Ayyo, mana koduku andi. Please me kallu mokutha. Paysal iyyandi. Treatment lekapothe chanipoye chance undi anta.

RAGHUNATH

Peda poyindi adu sachinaka. Nuvu kuda sachipo. Daridram vadulatadi naku. Naku ee paysal anna migultay.

SAROJINI

Ayyo, mana koduku andi. Please...

RAGHUNATH

Itlane na kala kada undali ee me bathukulu? Apudu meku paysal kani, food kani, bicham esta. Chepichuko po. Em treatment o.

Sarojini takes money from a chit fund. Runs to the doctor. Pays. Treatment starts.

She stays at the hospital for a few days.

Phone rings.

RAGHUNATH (on phone)

Eyyyy ne amma. Intiki ra. Ada doctor toni padukuntunava endi inni rojulu? Broker munda.

Sarojini says nothing. She needs his money. She goes home.

RAGHUNATH

Endi ee nee amma? Evadu pedtadu naku tindi? Hospital baga maza ichindu aa doctors andaru. Entha mandi deggara sapainchinav? Atla ne sampainchuko. Vee doc deggaranunchi an paysal enduku munda?

Sarojini bursts.

SAROJINI

Endi ra nuvu manishi putuka etla putinav ra lan...kodaka? Nee bathuku aa devudu enduku ichindu ra? Sontha koduku itla yate naku padukoni techuko antunav? Nuvu ra avvi ani chesesdi...
