
TITLE: ASHRAYAM
WRITTEN BY: NOOLI MANISH

FADE IN:

EXT. SLUM AREA – EVENING

A narrow alley buzzes with the sounds of life. A WOMAN in her 40s, SAROJ, returns home with grocery bags. Her son, ARJUN (21), well-groomed and disciplined, is fixing a torn book on the floor.

                         SAROJ
        One day you’ll rise above all this, Arjun. Just hold on.

He smiles. She’s tired, but proud.

INT. NIGHT SCHOOL – LATER

Arjun attends class. Afterward, he changes into a delivery uniform.

INT. STREET CORNER – NIGHT

Arjun meets PRIYA (19), his cheerful girlfriend. They share food, laugh. Their love is raw and innocent.

                         PRIYA
        I’ll love you even if the world ends.

EXT. ARJUN’S HOUSE – NIGHT

Boom. A deafening BLAST. Flames. Smoke.

INT. HOSPITAL – LATER

Arjun lies unconscious. His face is charred. His mother is covered in burns. Beeping. Priya rushes in, cries.

DAYS LATER – Saroj dies. Priya stands beside burned Arjun, unable to meet his eyes.

EXT. HOSPITAL GARDEN – DAY

Arjun, now out of bandages, sits alone. Priya approaches.

                         PRIYA (softly)
        I... I can’t do this anymore.

She walks away. Arjun’s world crumbles.

INT. SMALL ROOM – NIGHT

Arjun stares in the mirror. His once-kind eyes have hardened.

INT. BROTHEL – NIGHT

He walks into a room with MAYA (22), quiet, hesitant. She looks nervous.

                         ARJUN
        Don’t worry. I’m not here for that. I just... don’t have anyone.

She’s stunned. He sits on the floor. So does she.

MONTAGE – DAYS PASS

— They talk.  
— He brings her tea.  
— They laugh softly.

INT. BROTHEL – ONE NIGHT

                         ARJUN
        Why do you do this?

She turns away. The next day, she tells him everything.

                         MAYA
        We’re trapped. Owner has pictures... from when we were children. He’ll ruin us.

Arjun clenches his jaw.

EXT. BROTHEL – NIGHT

Arjun slips something into her drink – a sedative. Later, OWNER copies her pictures.

A BROTHEL WORKER sneaks into her room to assault her.

BANG! Arjun SHOOTS him in the thigh.

                         ARJUN (coldly)
        So you’ll remember pain every time you try.

INT. BROTHEL HALL – DAY

                         ARJUN
        I won’t beg. Come with me and live with dignity – or stay.

Several women step forward.

INT. ASHRAM – MONTHS LATER

A humble place. Clean. Kids playing. Women teaching. Maya, smiling, reads to orphans.

Arjun watches quietly. He remembers his mother.

                         SAROJ (V.O.)
        A mother doesn’t need a man. She needs strength.

TEXT ON SCREEN:

        “Some flames burn us. Others… light the path for the broken to become brave.”

FADE OUT.

THE END
