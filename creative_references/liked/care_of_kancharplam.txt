[REGISTER: hyper-realistic slice-of-life / gentle social drama]
[VIBE: Kancharapalem locality, non-actors, lived-in authenticity, quiet humor, dignity of labor, caste/religion as invisible walls, four love stories across ages]
[SETTING: Narrow lanes of Kancharapalem (Vizag), government office, wine shop, gym, school, railway tracks, temple]
[PACING: unhurried, observational, let the town breathe. Four parallel tracks that slowly converge.]
[TONE: tender, quietly funny, never heavy-handed. Heartbreak has tenderness. Messaging is embedded, not preached.]
[EMOTIONAL CORE: love as a universal human right, the invisible violence of "what will people say," faith lost and found in people]
